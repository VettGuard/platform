US carriers leave **billions** on the table every year because filing a detention claim takes 20 minutes per load and the paperwork beats the dispatcher's patience. We built VettGuard to make those 20 minutes turn into one click.

## What ships today

- **Chrome extension** that lives inside DAT and Truckstop — green/amber/red broker scores appear inline in the load board.
- **AI broker vetting** trained on 4.2M loads and payment outcomes from the carrier network.
- **Auto-detention** — once a dwell crosses 2 hours, the claim writes itself with BOL + ELD attached.

## The 18% success fee

We only get paid when **you get paid**. Detention recovery is free to start; we deduct 18% from the amount the broker actually pays. If they don't pay, we collect $0.
